Three directions for the NN mark

All three are original geometry built on a 76-unit grid, drawn as production SVG — not images. Pick one. It then becomes the locked identity across the dashboard, launcher, login, favicon and iPhone home screen, and is never changed again.

A

Interlock

structure · precision · permanence

Two N forms that share one stem — three verticals, two perfectly parallel diagonals. Nothing is decorative; every edge is on the grid. It reads unmistakably as NN at any size and feels engineered rather than styled. The strongest, most conservative choice, and the one that will still look right in twenty years.

B

Aperture

solidity · iconic · home screen

The NN is cut out of a solid tile rather than drawn as a glyph — a different register entirely. This is the most immediately iconic of the three and the only one that is already an app icon with no extra container: it will sit on your iPhone home screen as a single confident shape. Densest of the three, so it stays readable even at favicon size, and it is the hardest to mistake for anything else.

C

Monoline

calm · precise · spacious

One continuous line, uniform weight, bevelled joins instead of rounded or sharp ones — engineered rather than drawn. It has no start and no seam, which is literally the product: one unbroken environment that runs for a lifetime. The lightest and calmest of the three, the closest match to the "minimal, calm, spacious" brief, and it animates perfectly — the line can draw itself during the cinematic launch.
